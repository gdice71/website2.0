Crocheted Hats for the NICU

I debated putting this here because it is quieter and more personal than the rest of my work, but it matters to me.

I have been crocheting hats for babies at the local hospital NICU. I was born early at 29 weeks myself, so this feels like a small way to give something back.

Focus Softness, consistency, and making something useful by hand
Medium Hand-crocheted hats donated to the local hospital NICU
Why It Matters A personal way to give back to families starting where mine once did

Why this belongs here

This is not a technical project in the usual sense, but it still reflects the same things I value in my other work: patience, care in the details, and steady effort over time. I wanted to leave it in the archive because personal work can say something real too.

What guided it

  • Made for premature and newborn babies in the NICU
  • Built around softness, fit, and repeatable stitching
  • Included here because the quieter projects matter too
