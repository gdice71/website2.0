About

I don't settle for one thing. I never have.

I'm a mechanical engineer, but also an artist, a researcher, a builder, a teacher, and someone who has spent every stage of life trying to leave the room better than I found it.

At Shawnigan Lake School I built community and earned the Robertson Award, not by being the loudest, but by showing up fully and consistently for the people around me.

At Waterloo I taught 120 students how to think through hard problems, contributed to curriculum that will outlast my time there, and independently launched a CFD research project on synovial fluid dynamics in osteoarthritic knees because I was curious and nobody told me I couldn't.

I have passions that don't fit neatly into one discipline. Engineering taught me to think under constraint. Art taught me to see. Research taught me to stay with a question long enough for it to open up. Teaching taught me that clarity is the highest form of understanding.

I'm drawn to hard problems, precise execution, and work that actually matters to someone. The throughline across everything I build, whether it's a fluid simulation, a render, a web application, or something quieter, is that I care about doing it properly.

I am a builder. An artist. A thinker. Something of a renaissance woman, still very much in progress.
