About

Work shaped by discipline, not noise.

My background is grounded in technical precision and strong internal standards. I am drawn to problems where structure matters, details compound, and good decisions show up in the final finish.

I chose mechanical engineering because it teaches clear thinking under constraint. That mindset carries into everything I build.

I am most engaged in environments that reward rigor: complex surfaces, robotic systems, tightly resolved product details, and work that must perform as well as it looks. The goal is not visual excess. It is control, clarity, and a high standard of execution.
