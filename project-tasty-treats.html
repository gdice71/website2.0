Branding & Design

Tasty Treats Bake Stand

This branding piece was created for a Vancouver Island small business specializing in homemade baked goods. The visual goal was warmth without clutter, using regional cues and food illustration to make the identity immediately readable.

The final direction leaned on mountains, evergreens, sunrise imagery, and bakery motifs to keep the mark grounded in place while still feeling welcoming and commercial.

Focus Identity design with a rustic, local tone
Goal Communicate handmade quality and clear product association
Output Logo application and branded display materials

Design intent

The challenge here was not complexity. It was tone. The identity needed to feel approachable and handmade while still reading clearly in a real small-business setting. That meant limiting visual noise and making each illustration earn its place.

The result is warmer than the engineering work in this archive, but the same standard still applies: fewer elements, clearer hierarchy, stronger finish.
