Visualization

Lando Norris’ 1-of-1 MSO McLaren 765LT Spider

This project pushed further into cinematic rendering, combining studio sequences with a wet-asphalt environment to study how speed, reflection, and lighting can shape a more atmospheric image.

It also marked an early step into animation and motion work, using camera flow and pacing to make the car feel engineered rather than simply displayed.

Focus Motion, lighting, and cinematic automotive presentation
Tools Blender, animation, environmental rendering
Output Looped video studies and a hero still render

What changed from the first study

Compared with the P1 project, this one was less about clean static presentation and more about atmosphere. The scenes were designed to hold both precision and movement, using reflections, rain-dark surfaces, and camera rhythm to support the car’s personality.

That made it a stronger exercise in world-building. Instead of isolating the object, the project tested how context can sharpen the read of the design itself.

Key ideas

  • Light behavior across curved automotive forms
  • Motion and framing for a cinematic feel
  • Balancing detail with readability in darker scenes
