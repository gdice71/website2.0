3D Modeling

McLaren P1 Carbon Series

This was the start of a more serious automotive visualization direction in Blender, built around surface fidelity, lighting control, and the visual tension of a car that already feels in motion while standing still.

Each render was used to test how aggressive form, carbon detailing, and restrained red accents could read across different compositions without losing precision.

Focus Automotive rendering and clean surface presentation
Tools Blender, cinematic lighting, composition studies
Outcome 15-image portfolio study establishing a visual baseline

Why this project mattered

The objective was straightforward: make the form do the work. Instead of relying on overly dramatic styling, the study focused on proportion, reflections, and material contrast so the car’s engineering character could carry the image.

That constraint made it a useful portfolio exercise. It demanded cleaner camera decisions, more disciplined rendering choices, and a better understanding of what makes a technical object feel premium on screen.

What I was refining

  • Body surfacing and silhouette control
  • Material readability in carbon and painted surfaces
  • Lighting setups that emphasized structure over effects
